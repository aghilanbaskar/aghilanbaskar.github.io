A little bit about me

I'm a backend engineer who likes owning systems end to end, from high-level design and schema to vendor API contracts and rollout. I've spent 7+ years building systems where correctness matters: wallets, payments, payouts and notifications.

At mFine I lead a team of 2–4 backend engineers building RBI-compliant wallet and QR payment flows for 10M+ users. Before that, at HighLevel, I ran a 50M+ event notification service and owned the Meta and Google integrations for the CRM.

Lately I've been building AI agents with LangGraph and LangChain, and using Claude Code for day-to-day AI-assisted development across my team.
